常见问题

电台没有声音或一直加载

电台由各广播方直接提供，可能因网络、地区限制或对方调整而暂时不可用。请换一个电台试试；如果持续无法播放，可在显示屏上点按“电台反馈”告诉我们。

想听的电台没有收录

在未匹配到电台的位置点按显示屏，选择“补充电台”，填写电台名称或官网即可。

如何恢复购买

打开设置，点“恢复购买”。请确认登录的是购买时使用的 Apple 账户。

如何取消订阅

在设置中点“管理订阅”，或前往 iPhone“设置 › [你的名字] › 订阅”。取消后，当前周期结束前仍可使用完整版。

换新手机后收藏不见了

在两台设备上都开启 App 内的“iCloud 同步”，并登录同一个 Apple 账户。

版权与撤台请求

如果你是电台或节目的权利人，希望下架或更正某个电台，请发邮件说明电台名称和你的权利依据。核实后我们会尽快处理，并确保该电台不会被自动重新收录。

FAQ

A station has no sound or keeps loading

Stations are streamed directly by their broadcasters and may be temporarily unavailable because of the network, regional restrictions or changes on their side. Try another station; if it keeps failing, tap the display and choose Feedback.

A station I want is missing

Tap the display at a position with no station, choose Suggest, and enter the station name or website.

Restoring purchases

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you purchased with.

Cancelling a subscription

Tap Manage subscription in Settings, or go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone. You keep full access until the end of the current period.

My favorites are missing on a new phone

Turn on iCloud sync in the app on both devices and sign in with the same Apple Account.

Copyright and takedown requests

If you hold rights to a station or program and want it removed or corrected, email us with the station name and the basis of your rights. After verification we will act promptly and make sure it is not re-added automatically.
