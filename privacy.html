Atmo Radio 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日

Atmo Radio（以下称“本 App”）由个人开发者提供，即 App Store 页面显示的卖家。本政策说明本 App 处理哪些信息、为什么处理以及你可以如何控制。

概述

  • 本 App 不需要注册账户，不收集姓名、电话、通讯录或位置。
  • 本 App 不包含广告、统计分析或第三方追踪 SDK，也不跨 App 或网站追踪你。
  • 开发者不运营收集用户数据的服务器。

保存在你设备上的信息

收藏、收听历史、波段和频率记忆、外观、主题色、音效和触感等偏好保存在本机，用于恢复你的收音机状态。删除 App 会删除这些本机数据。系统整机备份是否包含这些数据，由你的 iOS 备份设置决定。

本 App 会在本机缓存电台目录和音频解码临时文件，并会定期按时间和剩余空间清理。

可选的 iCloud 同步

iCloud 同步默认关闭。你主动开启后，收藏、收听历史（最近 200 条）、偏好设置，以及恢复这些电台所需的电台名称和地址，会保存到你自己 Apple 账户的 iCloud 私有数据库（CloudKit）。开发者无法查看你的 iCloud 私有数据。

关闭同步会停止后续上传，已有云端数据保留。如需删除云端数据，可在 iPhone“设置 › [你的名字] › iCloud › 管理账户储存空间”中找到 Atmo Radio 并删除。

收听电台时的网络连接

播放电台时，你的设备会直接连接该电台或其流媒体服务商。和访问任何网站一样，对方可能收到你的 IP 地址、设备网络信息和请求时间。这些服务方按其自己的隐私政策处理信息，本 App 无法控制。

为更新电台目录，本 App 会在连接 Wi‑Fi 时向公开电台目录 Radio Browser（radio-browser.info）请求电台信息。请求内容是电台名称或编号，不包含你的个人信息，但对方同样能看到你的 IP 地址。

购买

订阅和买断由 Apple 处理。本 App 只读取 Apple 验证后的购买结果，用来判断你是否拥有完整版权限，不会获取你的付款方式或账单信息。

设备权限

  • 运动与方向：本 App 在本机读取手机倾斜角度，让按键、阴影和玻璃反光随角度变化。数据不保存、不上传。你可以在 App 设置中关闭“倾斜视差”。
  • 照片（仅添加）：只有在你主动开始录制、并选择保存时，本 App 才会把录制的 MP4 视频存入相册。本 App 不读取你的相册。录制不使用麦克风。

反馈邮件

当你使用“电台反馈”或“联系我们”时，本 App 会打开邮件，并预先填好当前电台、频率、播放状态和 App 版本。只有你在邮件中确认发送后，这些内容和你的邮箱地址才会发给开发者。开发者只用它来处理你的反馈，处理完毕后不会用于其他目的。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

你的权利

本机数据可以通过删除 App 清除，iCloud 数据可按上文方式删除。若你曾通过邮件联系开发者，可以发邮件要求查阅或删除邮件往来，开发者会在 30 天内回复。你所在地区的法律（例如 GDPR、英国 GDPR、CCPA/CPRA、加拿大 PIPEDA、澳大利亚和新西兰隐私法、日本个人信息保护法、中国个人信息保护法）可能赋予你更多权利，你可以通过下方联系方式提出。

政策变更

如本政策有实质变更，会更新本页面的生效日期，并在必要时于 App 内提示。

Atmo Radio Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Atmo Radio (“the app”) is provided by an individual developer, the seller shown on the App Store listing. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can control it.

Summary

  • The app requires no account and does not collect your name, phone number, contacts or location.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • The developer does not run any server that collects user data.

Information stored on your device

Favorites, listening history, band and frequency memory, and preferences such as appearance, theme color, sounds and haptics are stored on your device to restore your radio. Deleting the app deletes this data. Whether device backups include it depends on your iOS backup settings.

The app caches the station directory and temporary audio-decoding files on your device and clears them periodically based on age and free storage.

Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your favorites, listening history (the latest 200 entries), preferences, and the station names and addresses needed to restore them are stored in the private iCloud database (CloudKit) of your own Apple Account. The developer cannot see your private iCloud data.

Turning sync off stops further uploads and keeps existing iCloud data. To delete it, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on your iPhone, find Atmo Radio and delete its data.

Network connections while listening

When you play a station, your device connects directly to that broadcaster or its streaming provider. As with visiting any website, they may receive your IP address, network information and request time. They handle this under their own privacy policies, which the app does not control.

To update the station directory, the app requests station information from the public Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info) while on Wi‑Fi. Requests contain station names or IDs and no personal information, though Radio Browser can also see your IP address.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple. The app reads only Apple-verified purchase results to decide whether you have full access. It never receives your payment method or billing details.

Device permissions

  • Motion: the app reads your phone’s tilt on the device so keys, shadows and glass reflections respond to the angle. This data is neither stored nor uploaded. You can turn off Tilt parallax in the app’s settings.
  • Photos (add only): the app saves a recorded MP4 to Photos only after you start a recording yourself. It never reads your photo library. Recording does not use the microphone.

Feedback emails

When you use Station feedback or Contact us, the app opens an email pre-filled with the current station, frequency, playback state and app version. This content and your email address reach the developer only if you send the email. The developer uses it solely to handle your feedback.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

Your rights

You can clear on-device data by deleting the app and delete iCloud data as described above. If you have emailed the developer, you may ask to access or delete that correspondence, and the developer will respond within 30 days. Laws where you live (for example the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, the Australian and New Zealand Privacy Acts, Japan’s APPI and China’s PIPL) may give you further rights, which you can exercise using the contact below.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date on this page will be updated and, where appropriate, you will be notified in the app.
